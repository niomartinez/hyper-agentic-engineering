# General work continuity

Respect the current brief, organization rules and verified project scope. Retrieve only the preferences, decisions and source references needed for this task. Confirm volatile facts against their source.

At a milestone or cutoff, record the deliverable, decisions, evidence, open questions and next action. Use the project's existing handoff convention or a Markdown project brief in the working folder. Coding rules, repositories, branches and software tests are not required. Keep unrelated client and personal information out of shared work.
